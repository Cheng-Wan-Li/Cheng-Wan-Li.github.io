Research

Research directions

My work is organized around a shared question: how do geometry, information, and computation constrain what complex systems can do?

01

Quantum error correction & holographic codes

I study structural connections between quantum error correction and holographic codes, with an emphasis on how geometry and information constraints organize robust quantum systems.

  • quantum information
  • error correction
  • holographic codes
02

Hyperbolic geometry & fracton models

I am interested in the role of hyperbolic geometry in quantum many-body constructions, including models with restricted mobility and fracton-like structure.

  • hyperbolic geometry
  • fractons
  • many-body systems
03

Quantum speedups & machine learning

I investigate possible quantum advantages in learning and scientific computation, focusing on settings where the source of a speedup can be stated and analyzed precisely.

  • quantum algorithms
  • machine learning
  • computational advantage
04

AI for Science

I explore how machine learning can support scientific reasoning and discovery, particularly for physics-informed problems where structure and interpretability matter.

  • AI for Science
  • scientific machine learning
  • physics
