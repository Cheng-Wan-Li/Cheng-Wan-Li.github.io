Research directions

My work is organized around a shared question: how do geometry, information, and computation constrain what complex systems can do?

01

Quantum Error Correction

I study quantum error correcting codes including holographic codes and fracton codes, with an emphasis on how geometry and information constraints organize robust quantum systems.

  • quantum error correction
03

Quantum Algorithms & Learning

I investigate possible quantum advantages in learning and scientific computation, focusing on settings where the source of a speedup can be stated and analyzed precisely.

  • quantum algorithms
  • machine learning
  • computational advantage

AI for Science

I explore how AI can support scientific reasoning and discovery, particularly for physics-informed problems where structure and interpretability matter.
